Device ordering prototype

Opportunity statement

Bring device ordering, billing and settlement onto ODIN in one flow that works for any acquirer and any region. Victa is the first device line, Canada follows, and other acquirers can be added later.

Problem context

Device ordering doesn't happen on ODIN today. Each business unit has its own way of ordering devices, and its own way of billing merchants and settling the money for them.

Orders are passed by hand between a BU form, PayOps, POS Portal's site and Finance spreadsheets. Nobody has a single view of who ordered what, at what price, who paid, or where the money went. Merchants can't order or pay for devices themselves, so everything goes through a sales rep or Central Ops.

Impact

  • One way of working instead of many. Every BU orders, bills and settles devices the same way, in ODIN. Central Ops and Finance work from one record instead of chasing forms and spreadsheets.
  • Built once, reused everywhere. The flow doesn't depend on a specific acquirer or region. Victa on Worldpay in the US comes first. Canada and the in-person portfolio there come next. Later, other acquirers such as Stripe or Adyen can plug into the same flow without us rebuilding ordering each time.
  • ODIN grows beyond payments. ODIN starts to orchestrate the in-person business end to end: the device, the order, the payment and the settlement. This is a step towards ODIN being an orchestration platform, not just a payments platform.
  • Self-serve for merchants and reps. Merchants can order and pay in the portal, and reps stop filling in forms.

Desired outcome

  • All BUs order Victa devices through ODIN, with no separate BU processes, from closed beta in January.
  • From GA, merchants pay by card when they order, and billing and settlement for devices run through ODIN.
  • No orders re-keyed into POS Portal by the end of H1 2027.
  • Adding Canada, or a new acquirer, is a matter of adding its devices and fulfilment partner, not building a new ordering flow.

How we plan to get there

ODIN owns the order flow and payment. POS Portal owns inventory and shipping, so stock counts are out of scope (Graeme, 3 Nov).
